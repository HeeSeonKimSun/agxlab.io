Hee Seon Kim is Associate Professor in the Department of Game Contents and Animation, College of Arts, Chung-Ang University, and the principal investigator of AGX Lab. Her work spans animation, interactive media, and research on affect in games. Lab activities are listed on the [projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) pages.

## Screenings and talks

- **2025** Invited talk and screening, "Constructed Feeling: How Animation and Games Shape the Architecture of Emotion", Mooo 1.0 Students' Moving Image Festival, Anant National University, Ahmedabad, India (November 29).
- **2025** Screening and talk, "Having the Courage to Start a Revolution", Colloquium, Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM), Germany (January 21).
- **2023** Invited screening and discussion, University of Westminster, London, UK.
- **2021** Invited screening, Chitrakatha International Festival, National Institute of Design (NID), India.

## Exhibitions

- **2025** Solo exhibition, *As We Here*, Redbase Gallery, Sydney, Australia.
- **2023** Solo exhibitions, Saint George Gallery, London, and White Space Gallery, Edinburgh, UK.
- **2022** Invitational exhibition, National Assembly Members' Office Building, Seoul, Korea.
- **2020–2021** *Watangtangtongtang*, Buk Seoul Museum of Art, Seoul, Korea (art director).

## Commissioned and collaborative works

- **2022–2023** Google Arts & Culture × V&A Museum, UK (video technical supervisor).
- **2020** *Ultrashorts*, Samsung R&D Institute India (online screening).
- **2019** *Seasons of Jochang*, media facade project, Korea Creative Content Agency.
- **2017** *Retrospect Future*, collaborative project, Seoul Biennale of Architecture and Urbanism.
- **2015** *Interaction Orchestra with Media Facade*, performance, Royal College of Music, London, UK.

## Recognition

- **2015** *The River* selected as Best Indie Animation of 2015 by Sight & Sound, British Film Institute (BFI), and screened at the BFI.
