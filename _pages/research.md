## Research areas

### Affective–Cognitive Interaction in Games

Exploring how emotion and cognition interact to shape play, perception, and decision-making in interactive game environments through affective loops and behavioral modeling.

<p class="ko">게임 및 인터랙션 안에서 정서와 인지가 어떻게 플레이와 선택에 영향을 미치는지 연구합니다.</p>

### Embodied Interaction & Multimodal Analysis

Investigating embodied and predictive processes in gameplay using multimodal behavioral data, such as player choices, reaction time, and interaction patterns.

<p class="ko">플레이어의 행동, 반응, 생리 및 패턴 데이터를 통해 게임 경험을 측정하고 분석합니다.</p>

### Affective Experience and Media Interaction

Exploring how emotional experience is formed and communicated through narrative, animation, and interactive media to understand how affect shapes perception and meaning in digital environments.

<p class="ko">게임 세계와 내러티브, 애니메이션 속에서 정서와 선택이 어떻게 만들어지고 전달되는지를 탐구합니다.</p>

## Key research outputs

### Invited talks and lectures

- **Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM), Germany.** Invited talk on affective animation, exploring embodied emotional experience in protest environments in Korea.
- **Anant National University, India.** Invited lecture (scheduled), *Affect and Emotion: Embodied Experience*, on embodied affect and emotional experience in interactive and creative media contexts.
- **University of Westminster, UK.** Invited screening and discussion on affective animation and interactive storytelling, highlighting emotional design in digital narrative.
- **National Institute of Design (NID), India, Chitrakatha International Festival.** Invited screening of an animated short exploring rhythm, perception, and affective response in visual storytelling.

### Collaborative projects

- **NEXEN, Future Concept Mobility Collaboration.** Next-generation mobility systems encompassing autonomous robotics, user-inclusive vehicles, and a cross-domain mobility ecosystem.
- **DE RERUM NATURA, international collaborative development project.** Academy of Media Arts Cologne, Bauhaus University Weimar, China Central Academy of Fine Arts, National Institute of Design India, Accademia di Belle Arti di Napoli, El Palmaral Barcelona, and Chung-Ang University.
- **Google Arts & Culture × V&A Museum (UK–KR).** A collaborative video composition project developed for real-time audience interaction, integrating live visual synthesis, motion-based cues, and participatory media performance within immersive exhibition environments.
- **Ultrashorts Project, Samsung R&D Bangalore × NID India.** The affective and cognitive dimensions of ultra-short-form media, focusing on rhythm, perception, and emotional response in short interactive content.
- **Four Seasons, media facade project.** Korea Creative Content Agency & Gimpo Foundation for Arts and Culture. An interactive public media work transforming environmental data into emotional color mapping and dynamic architectural projection.
- **Retrospect Future, Seoul Biennale of Architecture & Urbanism × Yonsei University.** Collaborative exhibition examining memory and urban cognition through spatial storytelling and immersive visualization.
- **Butterfly Lover, interactive orchestra project, Royal College of Music, UK.** A cross-modal performance research project integrating emotional sonification, visual animation, and participatory audience interaction.

### Exhibitions and installations

- **National Assembly of the Republic of Korea.** Invited exhibition at the Members&rsquo; Hall, presenting research-based interactive animation and media installation integrating spatial experience and collective emotion.
- **Wadang-Tang Tung-Tung, Buk Seoul Museum of Art.** Interactive sound- and motion-based installation exploring collective words from artists with spatial experience in animation.

### Recognition

- **BFI (British Film Institute), UK.** Selected as Best Indie Animation and archived in the BFI National Archive collection for its experimental affective animation research output.
