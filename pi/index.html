Hee Seon Kim

Selected works by the principal investigator, 2015–2025. These predate or run alongside AGX Lab and are listed separately from the lab's own projects.

Hee Seon Kim is Associate Professor in the Department of Game Contents and Animation, College of Arts, Chung-Ang University, and the principal investigator of AGX Lab. Her work spans animation, interactive media, and research on affect in games. Lab activities are listed on the projects and publications pages.

Screenings and talks

  • 2025 Invited talk and screening, “Constructed Feeling: How Animation and Games Shape the Architecture of Emotion”, Mooo 1.0 Students’ Moving Image Festival, Anant National University, Ahmedabad, India (November 29).
  • 2025 Screening and talk, “Having the Courage to Start a Revolution”, Colloquium, Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM), Germany (January 21).
  • 2023 Invited screening and discussion, University of Westminster, London, UK.
  • 2021 Invited screening, Chitrakatha International Festival, National Institute of Design (NID), India.
