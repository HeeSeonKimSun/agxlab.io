AGX Lab

Affective Game Experience Lab · Chung-Ang University · 정서 게임 경험 연구실

College of Arts, Chung-Ang University

Da Vinci Campus, Anseong, Korea

heeseonkim@cau.ac.kr

We seek to explore how emotion and cognition interact within games and interactive media. Grounded in affective game computing and cognitive science, our research aims to understand how affective signals and cognitive processes shape play, decision-making, and multimodal experience through experimental and creative approaches.

우리는 게임과 애니메이션, 인터랙션 속에서 사람이 무엇을 느끼고 어떻게 생각하는지를 연구합니다. 플레이어가 경험하는 감정, 선택, 몰입의 순간들을 이해하고, 이를 바탕으로 더 풍부한 게임과 미디어 경험을 설계하는 방법을 탐구합니다.

research areas

Affective–Cognitive Interaction in Games

Exploring how emotion and cognition interact to shape play, perception, and decision-making in interactive game environments through affective loops and behavioral modeling.
게임 및 인터랙션 안에서 정서와 인지가 어떻게 플레이와 선택에 영향을 미치는지 연구합니다.

Embodied Interaction & Multimodal Analysis

Investigating embodied and predictive processes in gameplay using multimodal behavioral data, such as player choices, reaction time, and interaction patterns.
플레이어의 행동, 반응, 생리 및 패턴 데이터를 통해 게임 경험을 측정하고 분석합니다.

Affective Experience and Media Interaction

Exploring how emotional experience is formed and communicated through narrative, animation, and interactive media to understand how affect shapes perception and meaning in digital environments.
게임 세계와 내러티브, 애니메이션 속에서 정서와 선택이 어떻게 만들어지고 전달되는지를 탐구합니다.

join our lab

AGX Lab welcomes students and researchers interested in emotion, cognition, and interactive media. We seek to explore affective game computing and embodied experience through both creative and empirical approaches. No prior experience is required: you can grow from the basics through research and projects.

AGX Lab에 참여하세요! 감정, 인지, 게임과 애니메이션을 포함한 인터랙티브 미디어에 관심 있는 학생과 연구자를 모집합니다. 열정을 가진 학생이라면 누구나 환영하고, 사전 경험이 없어도 참여 가능하며, 연구와 프로젝트를 통해 기초부터 성장할 수 있습니다.

加入 AGX Lab！欢迎对情感、认知与交互媒体感兴趣的学生与研究者。研究方向包括情感游戏计算与具身体验。即使没有相关经验，也可以通过研究与项目从基础开始成长。

selected publications

  1. JKGS
    An Integrative Analysis of Games and Affect Research: Focusing on Affective Game Computing and Feedback Loop Types
    Hee Seon Kim
    Journal of Korea Game Society(게임과 정서 연구 통합 분석: 정서게임컴퓨팅 및 피드백 루프 유형을 중심으로, 한국게임학회 논문지 25(3)) , 2025
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    This study examines research trends in affective game computing through keyword network analysis and compares three feedback-loop types (cognition-based consequence loops, behavior-based affective loops, and physiological-signal-based affective game loops), analyzing their operating principles, characteristics, and technical limitations to provide an integrated framework for the field.

  2. JKGS
    A Study on Overlapped Configuration and Meta-simulation on Open-world
    Hee Seon Kim
    Journal of Korea Game Society(오픈 월드 게임의 중첩적 구성과 메타시뮬레이션 고찰, 한국게임학회 논문지 20(4)) , 2020
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Explores open-world games through Frasca’s notions of paidia and ludus, examining how open worlds overlap non-linear events with linear quests across a geographic world, and proposes meta-simulation as an alternative modeling tool that expands gameplay possibilities.
